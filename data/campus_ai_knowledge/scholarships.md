# Scholarship Policy

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional policy for demonstration only.

## Merit Scholarship

Students with a CGPA of 8.5 or above at the end of the previous academic year receive a merit scholarship of a 25% tuition fee waiver for the following year, provided there is no disciplinary action on record.

## Need-Based Scholarship

Students with an annual family income below 4 lakh rupees may apply for a need-based scholarship with an income certificate. Scholarship applications open in the first four weeks of the academic year.

## Scholarship Disbursement

Approved scholarships are adjusted against the next semester's fees. Questions about scholarship disbursement are raised as a Campus Services case in the fees category.
