# Campus Event Regulations

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional regulations for demonstration only.

## Event Registration

Students register for campus events through the campus portal before the registration deadline. Event registration closes when the event reaches its capacity.

## Events, Classes and Examinations

Event participation does not excuse a student from a scheduled class or examination unless on-duty (OD) permission is approved by the class faculty member in advance.

## Club Events

Club events must be approved by the Dean of Students Office at least seven days in advance and must end by 22:00 on campus.
