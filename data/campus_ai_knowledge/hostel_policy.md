# Hostel Policy

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional policy for demonstration only.

## Hostel Room Allocation

Hostel rooms are allocated by the Hostel Office at the start of each academic year based on year of study and availability. Hostel room change requests are reviewed in order of submission.

## Hostel Timings and Visitors

Hostel residents must sign in through the hostel register after 22:00. Visitors are allowed only in the hostel common areas between 09:00 and 19:00, with prior intimation to the warden.

## Hostel Fees

Hostel fees are billed per semester with tuition fees. A resident vacating the hostel mid-semester must obtain a no-dues clearance from the Hostel Office.

## Hostel Discipline

Ragging of any kind is strictly prohibited in the hostel and is reported to the Dean of Students Office immediately.
