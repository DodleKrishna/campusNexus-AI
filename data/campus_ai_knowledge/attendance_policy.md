# Attendance Policy

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional policy for demonstration only.

## Minimum Attendance Requirement

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each enrolled course for a semester to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination in that course. Internal assessments and quizzes are not withheld for attendance shortage.

## Calculation Method

Attendance percentage is calculated per course as classes attended divided by classes conducted, rounded to one decimal place. A late arrival recorded by the faculty member counts as attended. An excused absence does not count as attended.

## Attendance Shortage and Condonation

A student between 65% and 75% attendance in a course may apply to the Academic Office for condonation with a medical certificate or another documented reason. Condonation is decided by the Dean of Academics. Condonation below 65% is not granted.

## Leave and On-Duty

Approved on-duty (OD) participation in institution events is recorded by the class faculty member. Medical leave must be requested within three working days of returning to class through the leave request workflow.
