# Placement Policy

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional policy for demonstration only.

## Placement Eligibility

Students in their third and final year may register with the Placement Cell. Each placement opportunity states its minimum CGPA, eligible departments and eligible years; a student may apply only when all of them are met.

## One Offer Rule

A student who accepts a full-time placement offer is not eligible to apply for further full-time placements in the same academic year. Internships do not count towards this rule.

## Placement Applications

Placement applications are submitted through the campus portal before the opportunity deadline. Application status is updated by the Placement Cell as submitted, under review, accepted or rejected.

## Interview Conduct

Students must attend every interview round they register for. Missing a scheduled round without informing the Placement Cell suspends placement registration for 30 days.
