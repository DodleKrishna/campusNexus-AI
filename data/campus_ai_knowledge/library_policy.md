# Library Policy

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional policy for demonstration only.

## Library Borrowing Limits

Undergraduate students may borrow up to four library books at a time for 14 days. A loan may be renewed once if no other student has reserved the book.

## Library Overdue Fines

An overdue library book is fined 5 rupees per day. A student with library fines above 200 rupees cannot borrow until the fine is paid.

## Library Hours

The central library is open from 08:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to 17:00 on Saturdays. During examination weeks the library reading hall stays open until midnight.
