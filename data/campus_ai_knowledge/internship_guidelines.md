# Internship Guidelines

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional guidelines for demonstration only.

## Who May Apply for an Internship

Students from the second year onwards may take internships. Semester-time internships require the department's approval and must not conflict with scheduled classes or examinations.

## Internship Credits

An internship of at least six weeks with a completion certificate and a report evaluated by the department earns two elective credits.

## Internship Approval Process

Students submit the internship offer letter to the department internship coordinator before joining. Remote internships follow the same approval process.
