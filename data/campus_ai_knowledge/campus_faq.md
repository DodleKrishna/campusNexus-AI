# Campus FAQ

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional answers for demonstration only.

## Campus Wi-Fi

Students connect to the campus Wi-Fi with their institution ID and portal password. Wi-Fi problems are reported as a Campus Services case in the IT category.

## ID Card Replacement

A lost ID card is replaced by the Administration Office within two working days for a fee of 300 rupees.

## Medical Help

The campus Health Centre is open 24 hours. Emergency ambulance service is available through the campus security desk.

## Fee Payment

Semester fees are paid online through the campus portal before the due date announced by the Accounts Office. A late payment fee applies after the due date.
