# Assignment Submission Rules

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional rules for demonstration only.

## Submission Deadline

An assignment is on time if it is submitted at or before its published deadline. The deadline shown in the campus portal is authoritative.

## Late Submission

A submission after the deadline is recorded as late. Late work is accepted up to 48 hours after the deadline with a 20% reduction of the marks awarded. After 48 hours a late submission is accepted only with the written approval of the course faculty member.

## Assignment Reminders

Students who have not submitted an assignment are reminded through the campus app according to the institution communication policy. Reminders are not sent during quiet hours from 22:00 to 07:00.

## Academic Integrity

Submitted assignment work must be the student's own. Copied work receives zero marks and is reported to the department.
