# Examination Regulations

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional regulations for demonstration only.

## Eligibility for End-Semester Examinations

A student is eligible to write the end-semester examination of a course only if the attendance requirement of the Attendance Policy is met for that course and there are no pending fee dues. Hall tickets are issued only to eligible students.

## Midterm, Internal Assessment and Quiz Rules

Midterms, internal assessments and quizzes are conducted by the course faculty member during the semester. Students must carry their institution ID card. Arriving more than 15 minutes after the start is not permitted.

## Missed Examinations and Makeup

A student who misses an internal assessment or quiz for a documented medical or approved on-duty reason may request a makeup within 7 days. Makeup requests are decided by the course faculty member. No makeup is offered for an unexplained absence.

## Malpractice

Any use of unauthorized material or devices in an examination is treated as malpractice and reported to the Examination Committee, which decides the penalty.
