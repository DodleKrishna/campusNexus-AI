# Hostel Complaint Procedure

CAMPUS AI demo institution document. Fictional procedure for demonstration only.

## How to File a Hostel Complaint

A hostel complaint (plumbing, electrical, furniture, cleanliness, mess food or Wi-Fi in the hostel) is filed as a Campus Services case in the hostel category, with the room number and a short description. Each hostel complaint gets a case code for tracking.

## Hostel Complaint Response Times

Hostel complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours. Urgent hostel issues (no water, no electricity, safety) are resolved within 24 hours; other hostel complaints within 72 hours.

## Hostel Complaint Escalation

If a hostel complaint is not resolved within its response time, it is escalated to the Chief Warden. If it is still unresolved after a further 48 hours, it is escalated to the Dean of Students Office.

## Closing a Hostel Complaint

A hostel complaint is closed only after the resident confirms the fix in the campus portal, or does not respond within 3 days of the resolution notice.
