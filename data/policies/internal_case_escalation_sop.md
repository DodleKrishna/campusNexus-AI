# Internal Case Escalation SOP

## Escalation Trigger

Campus Services staff escalate a case internally when it breaches its resolution SLA, per the Grievance SLA Policy, or when a student requests manual escalation.

## Escalation Path

Escalations route to the department head of the owning office: Hostel Office, IT Helpdesk, Accounts Office, Administrative Office, or Facilities Office. Cases unresolved 48 hours after escalation route further to the Dean of Students Office.

## Internal Reporting

Each office reports weekly SLA-breach counts to the Dean of Students Office. This SOP and its reporting thresholds are for staff use and are not published to students.
