# Examination Regulations

## Eligibility to Appear for Examinations

A student is eligible to appear for the end-semester examination in a course only if they meet the minimum attendance requirement in force for that semester, per the Attendance Policy, and have cleared all outstanding fee dues.

## Hall Ticket Procedure

Hall tickets are generated automatically ten days before the first examination date once eligibility is confirmed, and are downloadable from the student portal. Students with an unresolved fee or attendance hold must contact the Accounts Office or Academic Office before a hall ticket can be issued.

## Malpractice and Grievances

Any suspected examination malpractice is reported to the Controller of Examinations for inquiry. A student may file a grievance regarding an examination result within 15 days of result publication, per the Grievance SLA Policy.

## Re-evaluation Requests

Re-evaluation of an answer script may be requested within 7 days of result publication, subject to a re-evaluation fee set each semester by the Controller of Examinations.
