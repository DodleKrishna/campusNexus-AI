# Library Policy

## Borrowing Procedure

Students may borrow up to 5 books at a time using their student ID card at the library circulation desk. The loan period is 14 days for undergraduate students and 21 days for postgraduate students, renewable once if no reservation is pending on the title.

## Late Return Fines

A fine of 2 currency units per book per day applies after the due date. Fines above 500 currency units block further borrowing until cleared.

## Reference and Digital Resources

Reference-section books and periodicals do not circulate and must be used within the library. Digital journals and e-books are accessible off-campus through the library portal using student credentials.

## Lost or Damaged Books

A lost or significantly damaged book is charged at its replacement cost plus a processing fee, and must be reported to the Library Office as soon as the loss is discovered.
