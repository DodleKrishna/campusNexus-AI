# Placement Policy

## Eligibility for Campus Placement

Final-year students with a CGPA of 6.0 or above and no outstanding disciplinary case are eligible to register with the Career Services Office for campus placement drives.

## Registration Procedure

Eligible students register through the placement portal at the start of the placement season and upload an updated resume. Registration remains open until the Career Services Office announces closure for a given drive.

## Number of Offers

A student may accept at most one core placement offer through the campus process. Accepting an offer withdraws the student from further core drives for that placement season, though dream and super-dream drives announced separately remain open.

## Conduct During Drives

Students must attend all stages of a drive they register for. Withdrawing after being shortlisted for the final round without a valid reason may result in debarment from placement activities for one academic year.
