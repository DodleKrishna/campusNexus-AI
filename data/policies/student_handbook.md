# Student Handbook

## Welcome

This handbook is a general reference for Meridian University students, summarizing policies covered in more detail in the Attendance Policy, Examination Regulations, Placement Policy, Internship Policy, Campus Events and Registration Policy, Grievance SLA Policy, Hostel Policy, Scholarship Policy, and Library Policy documents.

## Event Registration Procedure

As a general campus rule, students register for events through the student portal at least 24 hours before the registration deadline shown for that event. Individual departments may publish their own circulars with additional or stricter requirements for events they organize.

## Code of Conduct

Students are expected to engage respectfully with faculty, staff, and fellow students. Academic dishonesty, harassment, and ragging are subject to disciplinary action through the Dean of Students Office.

## Getting Help

Academic questions go to the Academic Office; career and internship questions go to Career Services; hostel, fees, facilities, and IT issues are filed as a Campus Services case; grievances follow the Grievance SLA Policy.
