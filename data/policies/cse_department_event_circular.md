# CSE Department Circular - Event Participation Requirements

## Event Registration Procedure for CSE Students

Computer Science & Engineering students registering for any technical event, workshop, or competition -- whether club-organized or office-organized -- must complete registration at least 48 hours before the event's registration deadline, in place of the 24-hour window stated in the general Campus Events and Registration Policy. This extended window allows the department to review participation for events held during class hours.

## Off-Campus Events

CSE students participating in an off-campus event or competition require written approval from the CSE Head of Department at least one week in advance, in addition to any registration completed through the student portal.
