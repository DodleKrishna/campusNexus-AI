# Grievance Redressal and SLA Policy

## Filing a Grievance

Students may file a grievance or service request -- hostel, fees, facilities, IT helpdesk, or administrative -- through the Campus Services portal. Each request is logged as a case with a category, priority, and assigned department.

## Response and Resolution SLA

Every case receives a first response within the SLA window for its priority: 2 hours for Urgent, 4 hours for High, 24 hours for Normal, and 48 hours for Low priority cases. Full resolution targets, measured from case creation, are 24 hours for Urgent, 24 hours for High, 120 hours for Normal, and 168 hours for Low priority cases.

## Escalation

A case that breaches its resolution SLA without being resolved is automatically flagged for escalation review. Students may also request manual escalation by replying to the case with "escalate" through the portal.

## Academic-Impact Cases

Cases that block an academic process, such as a fee discrepancy preventing hall ticket generation, are treated as at least High priority regardless of the category the student originally selected.
