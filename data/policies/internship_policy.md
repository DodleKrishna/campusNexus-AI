# Internship Policy

## Eligibility for Internships

Students in their third year or above may apply for internships listed through the Career Services Office, provided their CGPA meets the minimum threshold specified for each individual internship opportunity and they meet that opportunity's department and skill requirements.

## Academic Credit for Internships

An internship of at least 6 weeks' duration may be submitted for academic credit if approved in advance by the student's department coordinator. Internship completion certificates and a supervisor evaluation must be submitted within 2 weeks of completion.

## Internship and Attendance

Students undertaking a credit-bearing internship during the semester are granted an attendance exemption for the internship period, which must be pre-approved and recorded with the Academic Office so it does not affect the course attendance calculation.

## Stipend and Offer Letters

Any stipend, offer letter, or internship agreement is between the student and the employer; the university does not co-sign internship agreements. Students should route employer communication regarding offers through the Career Services Office for record-keeping.
