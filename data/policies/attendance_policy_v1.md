# Attendance Policy (2023-24)

## Minimum Attendance Requirement

Students must maintain a minimum of 70% attendance in each enrolled course for a semester to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination in that course. Attendance is tracked per course, not as a single campus-wide average.

## Attendance Shortage Condonation

Students falling between 60% and 70% attendance in a course may apply for condonation through the Academic Office, supported by a medical certificate or other documented reason. Condonation below 60% is not granted under this policy.

## Calculation Method

Attendance percentage is calculated as classes attended divided by classes conducted, per course, rounded to one decimal place. Classes conducted excludes days the course was officially cancelled by the instructor.
