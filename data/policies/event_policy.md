# Campus Events and Registration Policy

## Event Registration Procedure

Students register for any campus event through the student portal at least 24 hours before the event's registration deadline. Registration is confirmed only if the event's capacity has not been reached; students registering after capacity is reached are placed on a waitlist.

## Timetable and Exam Conflicts

Students should not register for events that overlap their timetable class slots or a scheduled examination. The portal does not automatically block conflicting registrations, so students are responsible for checking their own calendar before registering.

## Cancellations

A student may cancel an event registration up to 6 hours before the event start time through the portal. Cancelling after that window should be reported to the organizing club or office directly.

## Club-Organized vs Office-Organized Events

Some events are organized by student clubs and others directly by a campus office, such as the Dean of Students Office or the Career Services Office. Individual departments may publish their own circulars setting additional requirements for events organized within that department, in addition to this general policy.
