# Hostel Policy

## Room Allocation and Reallocation

Hostel rooms are allocated at the start of each academic year by the Hostel Office based on year of study and availability. Reallocation requests are submitted as a Campus Services case under the "hostel" category and are reviewed in order of submission.

## Maintenance Requests

Maintenance issues such as plumbing, electrical, or furniture problems are reported as a Campus Services case under the "hostel" category with a description and room number, and are handled per the Grievance SLA Policy.

## Hostel Rules

Residents must sign in and out through the hostel register after 10 PM. Guests are permitted in common areas only, with prior intimation to the hostel warden. Ragging of any kind is strictly prohibited and is escalated to the Dean of Students Office immediately.

## Fee and Vacancy

Hostel fees are billed per semester along with tuition fees. A student vacating a hostel room mid-semester must submit a no-dues clearance from the Hostel Office before the room is reassigned.
