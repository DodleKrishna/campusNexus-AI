# Attendance Policy (2025-26)

## Minimum Attendance Requirement

Effective the 2025-26 academic year, students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each enrolled course for a semester to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination in that course. This supersedes the 70% requirement that applied under the 2023-24 policy.

## Attendance Shortage Condonation

Students falling between 65% and 75% attendance in a course may apply for condonation through the Academic Office, supported by a medical certificate or other documented reason, at the Dean of Academics' discretion. Condonation below 65% is not granted under this policy.

## Calculation Method

Attendance percentage is calculated as classes attended divided by classes conducted, per course, rounded to one decimal place. This calculation method is unchanged from the prior policy version.
