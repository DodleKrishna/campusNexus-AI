# Scholarship Policy

## Merit Scholarship Eligibility

Students with a CGPA of 8.0 or above at the end of an academic year, and no pending disciplinary case, are eligible to apply for the Merit Scholarship for the following year.

## Need-Based Scholarship Eligibility

Need-based scholarships require a family income certificate submitted through the Accounts Office along with the scholarship application, in addition to maintaining a minimum CGPA of 6.0.

## Application Window

Scholarship applications open at the start of each academic year and close 30 days later. Late applications are not accepted except by written exception from the Dean of Students Office.

## Disbursement

Approved scholarship amounts are disbursed to the student's fee account within 45 days of approval. Disbursement delays should be raised as a Campus Services case under the "fees" category.
